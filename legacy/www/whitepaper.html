1. Abstract

The traditional education market is plagued by three core inefficiencies: low completion rates, unverifiable credentials, and a lack of direct financial incentives for learners. L2Earn introduces a new paradigm: Learn-to-Earn. By integrating the FH-DREAM cognitive engine with a localized blockchain consensus layer, we allow users to mint "Knowledge Blocks" that serve as proof of skill, unlocking direct liquidity from educational grants and corporate recruiting pools.

The Problem

  • • 3% Completion Rates: MOOCs fail to retain users due to passive consumption.
  • • Credential Inflation: Certificates are easily forged or obtained without true mastery.
  • • Illiquid Knowledge: Years of study do not translate to value until a job is secured.

The Solution

  • • Proof of Knowledge (PoK): AI-verified, cryptographic proof of mastery.
  • • Liquid Skills: Earn $L2E tokens immediately upon mastering a module.
  • • FH-DREAM Engine: A cognitive framework that guarantees deep understanding, not just rote memorization.

2. Proof of Knowledge (PoK) Consensus

Unlike traditional "Learn to Earn" models that pay for mere attendance, L2Earn utilizes a Proof of Knowledge mechanism.

// The PoK Lifecycle 1. Learner -> Submits Insight/Quiz (Step D in FH-DREAM) 2. AI Node (Gemini) -> Validates semantic depth & uniqueness. 3. Oracle Network -> Cross-references with market data. 4. Smart Contract -> Mints KnowledgeBlock(SBT) + Releases $L2E.

3. Tokenomics ($L2E)

Utility

  • Governance: Vote on new curriculum tracks.
  • Access: Burn $L2E to access "Creator Studio" AI tools.
  • Staking: Creators stake $L2E to publish courses. Slashing occurs if content is flagged as low quality.

Distribution

Learn-to-Earn Pool 40%
Creator Fund 30%
DAO Treasury 20%

4. Technical Architecture

Layer 1: Identity & Data (Off-chain)

Supabase manages user profiles, rich media content, and real-time interaction states. Authentication handles Web2 (Email) and Web3 (Wallet) login mapping.

Layer 2: The Cognitive Engine (AI)

Google Gemini API powers the FH-DREAM engine, acting as the primary validator for "soft skills" that traditional code cannot verify.

Layer 3: Settlement (On-chain)

Optimism / Arbitrum (L2) handles the micro-transactions of $L2E tokens and the minting of Soulbound Tokens (SBTs) for course completion.
